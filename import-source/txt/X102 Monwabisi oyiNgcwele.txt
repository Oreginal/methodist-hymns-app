1.Monwabisi oyiNgcwele,
Mthombo wazo izibele,
Hlis' umbethe wakho.
Yiva ukukhanga kwethu,
Utyebis' umxhelo wethu,
Ngokunyela kwaKho.
[ENG]Celebrate the Holy 
who enriches our hearts.[/ENG]

2.Sonwabise thina sonke,
Usivis' uxolo lonke
Ngokunqhina kwaKho.
Sikhokel' enyanisweni,
Usimise entandweni,
Ngokusiza kwaKho.
[ENG]Rejoice as we come into
His peaceful presence.[/ENG]

3.Sothi, salathiswe nguWe,
Side sifikela kuYe
umSindisi wethu;
Sibubon' ubuso baKhe,
Ekhanyeni lethu. Amen
[ENG]Through the saviour we
come to him to see his face.[/ENG]
Amen.
