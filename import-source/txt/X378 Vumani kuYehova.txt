1 Vumani kuYehova

Nibong' igama laKhe;

Nilondolozwe nguYe,

Ma nenz' udumo IwaKhe.

[ENG]

Give thanks to God 

He has preserved us[/ENG]



2 Kufik' umnyaka omtjha,

Omdala se umkile;

Sisekho ebomini,

Uninzi se lufile.

Ma singazilibali

[ENG]

In this new year we 

are a new creation.[/ENG]



3 Ezo nto sizamkele,

Ngomnyak' ogqithileyo,

NgobaKhe ububele.

Nezoono soona ngazo, 

Phakathi kwexa lawo;

[ENG]

Put away the sins 

of the past.[/ENG]



4 Sicela uxolelo,

Zimkiswe kwa nje ngawo.

Ngalo uvelileyo,

Kwa yonk' imihla yawo,

Ma senz' inkonzo yethu 

Ilunge kuWe, Bawo.

[ENG]

Receive forgiveness

and move on to serve the Lord[/ENG]



6. Ma singakuqumbisi

Ngokwenza sikwenzayo;

Ma singakudanisi

Ngezimvo sizivayo.

[ENG]

Let go of resentment[/ENG]



7. Sithemb' uthando IwaKho

Wasiph' uYesu ngalo;

Sincede ngenxa yaKhe

Ixesha sisenalo.

[ENG]Trust in God's love

Receive God's gift of time.[/ENG]



8. Sandule, lakuphela,

Sicande emafini,

Sinyuke nomSindisi,

Sihlale ezulwini. 

[ENG]

One day we will 

join him in the clouds of heaven.[/ENG]

Amen.
