Rapellang, Jerusalema,

A abeloe khotso.

Ba ratang Thaba Sione,

Ba tla ja leseho.

[ENG] Pray for the peace of Jerusalem. [/ENG]

Khotso e ke hlolehe,

E ate ho eona;

Nala e kholo e hlahe

Ka tlung ea Morena.

[ENG]Peace upon all of us.[/ENG]

Lumelang, bana ba Ntate,

Ba keneng tempeleng!

Molimo o le ruise

Molem'o sa feleng. 

[ENG]In faith we are God's children.[/ENG]

Amen
