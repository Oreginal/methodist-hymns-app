Ndincede so-bungcwele,
Onaw' amandla onke;
Ze ndilushamayele
Kwa semhlabeni wonke,
Udumo lomKhululi wethu,
Osusa zonk' izoono zethu..
[ENG]Make me Holy[/ENG]

Ngegama laKho, Yesu!
Kudamba ukoyikal
Ngofefe lwaKho, Yesu!
Uxolo luya fika;
Kuphel' iintlungu zeentliziyo,
Kuvuk' ubom' obuhleliyo.
[ENG]Give me peace[/ENG]
Wasiqhaw'lela, Yesu!
Kwimiphefumlo yethu,
Onk' amakhamandela
Anzima phezu kwethu;
Enguwe osisindisile,
Sihambe sikhululekile. 
Amen.
